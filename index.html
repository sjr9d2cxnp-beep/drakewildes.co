How I run discovery

Same structure I use on incomplete signals. Applied to a sales conversation.

1. Detect Separate the reported symptom from what the system is actually showing.
2. Validate Confirm the constraint with evidence before recommending a next step.
3. Right-size Propose the smallest action that moves the evaluation forward.
4. Quantify Frame impact in dwell, parts cost, and residual exposure.
5. Residual risk State what the platform addresses, what remains open, and what to watch.
